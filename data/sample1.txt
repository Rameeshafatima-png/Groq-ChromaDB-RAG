Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence, commonly called AI, is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI systems can learn from data, recognize patterns, understand language, and make decisions. Common examples of AI include virtual assistants, recommendation systems, chatbots, and image recognition.

Machine learning is an important part of artificial intelligence. Machine learning allows computers to learn patterns from data without being explicitly programmed for every task.

AI is used in many areas including healthcare, education, finance, transportation, customer service, and entertainment.

The main goal of artificial intelligence is to develop systems that can process information and perform useful tasks efficiently.
